### Step 2: GSM8K quick eval (M0 vs M3)

```bash
CKPT_M0=checkpoints/codec_qwen3_original_full.pt
CKPT_M3=checkpoints/codec_qwen3_ijepa_full_data.pt

for task in gsm8k arc_c gpqa; do
    for pair in "$CKPT_M0:original" "$CKPT_M3:ijepa"; do
        ckpt="${pair%%:*}"; label="${pair##*:}"
        python3 run.py --method vision_latent_mas_codec_new \
            --model_name model_download/Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking \
            --vision_codec_path "$ckpt" --task "$task" \
            --max_samples 50 --generate_bs 1 --latent_steps 1024 \
            --max_new_tokens 512 --run_dir "runs/day1_${label}_${task}" \
            2>&1 | tee "runs/day1_${label}_${task}.log"
    done
done
```

## Go/No-Go 决策标准

### GO（绿灯）
- I-JEPA全量Eff Rank > 30
- I-JEPA/Original Eff Rank ratio > 5x
- GSM8K上I-JEPA不低于Original（容许平手）

### YELLOW（黄灯，继续但降低预期）
- Eff Rank 10-30，GSM8K持平
- 叙事改为"JEPA显著缓解但未完全解决"

### NO-GO（红灯，转向）
- I-JEPA全量Eff Rank < 10
- 转向纯诊断论文："蒸馏维度坍塌：发现、诊断和局限"
